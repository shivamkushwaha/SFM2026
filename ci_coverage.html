How to run this in class.

  • Draw one interval at a time about ten times. Ask the class, each time, whether this particular interval contains μ. They can see the answer only because the simulation cheats and shows μ.
  • Then draw 1000. The observed coverage settles on the nominal level. That number, and not the single interval, is what the confidence level describes.
  • Switch to 68 percent for contrast. Roughly a third of the lines turn red, and the point lands hard.
  • Set the population to skewed and n to 5. Coverage falls below the nominal level, because the central limit theorem has not had enough observations to work with.
