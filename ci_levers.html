Width against sample size

At the current σ and confidence level. The marker shows where your chosen n sits on the curve.

Three demonstrations.

  • Hold n and σ fixed and step the confidence level from 90 to 99 percent. The interval widens by about 57 percent. The data did not change at all, only the certainty demanded.
  • Hold everything else and quadruple n, from 25 to 100. The width halves exactly. Then quadruple again to 400 and watch how much less the second doubling of effort achieves.
  • Push σ up. The interval widens and nothing in the study design can prevent it, which is why a variable population needs a larger sample for the same precision.
