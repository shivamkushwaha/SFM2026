What to watch. The left panel is fixed. The middle panel keeps roughly the shape of the left panel however large n becomes. Only the right panel narrows, and it narrows towards μ at the rate σ divided by √n.

Draw 200 samples at n = 2, then push n to 50 and reset. The observed standard error should track the theoretical value closely once a few hundred samples have accumulated.
